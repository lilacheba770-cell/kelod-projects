Common questions

What buyers
ask us most

How long does it take from purchase to moving in?

On our projects the range is 14–24 months, depending on the size of the project and the stage at which you joined. The date is set in the contract and we commit to it — so far we have met it on every project we have delivered.

How is the payment schedule structured?

Payments follow the actual pace of construction rather than fixed dates: a deposit on signing, then payments at the frame, envelope, finishing and handover stages. Every payment is backed by a sale-law guarantee.

Can we make changes to the apartment?

Yes. Until the end of the frame stage you can change the internal layout, electrical and plumbing points and the finish specification. We price every change clearly in advance, with no surprises on the final bill.

What happens if defects appear after handover?

Every apartment comes with a full warranty year and cover on systems and waterproofing under the sale law. You go to the same point of contact who guided you through the project — not an external call centre.

Can we visit a completed project?

Absolutely, and it is what we recommend most. We will gladly arrange a visit to an occupied project and put you in touch with residents who have lived there for a few years.
